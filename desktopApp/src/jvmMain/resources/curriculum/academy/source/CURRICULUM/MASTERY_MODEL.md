# Mastery and Practice Model

## Objective score

Store a 0-100 mastery score per learner and objective. The score is evidence-based and separate from lesson completion.

Suggested evidence weights:

- independent correct response: strong positive evidence;
- correct after a small hint: moderate positive evidence;
- correct after solution reveal: completion evidence but little mastery evidence;
- incorrect response tied to a known misconception: targeted negative evidence;
- successful delayed review: strong retention evidence;
- project rubric evidence: high-value transfer evidence.

## Unlocking

- Lessons are always reviewable after completion.
- Normal next nodes unlock at mastery 70.
- Checkpoints and projects require prerequisite objectives at mastery 80.
- Placement tests can mark objectives as demonstrated while still scheduling a later confirmation review.

## Review schedule

Use an adaptive schedule similar to 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, then longer. Shorten the interval after errors or heavy hint dependence. Never punish a learner by removing content access.

## Session composition

Recommended adaptive session: 50 percent due review, 25 percent weak skills, 15 percent current unit, 10 percent challenge or transfer. Explain why each item was selected.

## Integrity

XP, streaks, and progress change only after persisted qualifying events. Opening a screen, rapidly clicking, or revealing an answer does not count as mastery.
