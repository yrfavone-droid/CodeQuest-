# Product Requirements - CodeQuest AI Academy

## Vision

CodeQuest AI Academy turns curiosity into measurable AI engineering ability. It keeps the existing "Learn. Build. Improve." identity while moving from broad coding instruction to a coherent AI pathway.

## Primary learners

1. Absolute beginners who need Python and school-level math refreshers.
2. Intermediate programmers who need mathematics, ML, and real projects.
3. Advanced learners who need deep learning, LLMs, vision, RL, evaluation, deployment, and responsible AI.

## Jobs to be done

- Tell me exactly what to learn next.
- Let me practice until I can solve independently.
- Let me write and run real code safely.
- Explain why my answer failed and connect it to a misconception.
- Help me turn lessons into portfolio projects.
- Let me read deeper material without blocking the main learning path.
- Show evidence of mastery, not inflated activity metrics.

## Success metrics

- activation: diagnostic or first lesson completed;
- learning: mastery-check improvement and review retention;
- engagement: weekly verified practice and project work;
- quality: content defect rate, test failures, disputed answers, duplicate rate;
- progression: track and checkpoint completion;
- reliability: editor execution success, sync success, and crash-free sessions;
- integrity: percentage of metrics based on real stored learner actions.

## MVP versus full release

MVP: migration-safe foundation, new navigation and dashboard, tracks T01-T06, Python editor, Math Lab basics, adaptive practice, content studio, one complete book, five deep dives, and at least 2,000 validated problems.

Full release: all 12 tracks, all five books, all 20 deep dives, all 10,000 validated problems, all labs, capstone system, advanced progress analytics, and production monitoring.

## Exclusions

- No copied brand assets or visual clone of another AI product.
- No unsafe unrestricted code execution.
- No automatic publishing of unreviewed AI-generated content.
- No deletion of legacy content or progress without explicit owner approval and backup.
- No fake learner counts, fake testimonials, or simulated completion data.
